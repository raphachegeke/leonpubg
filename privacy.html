3. Information We Handle

Because this website has no forms or database, the only personal information I receive is what you choose to send me. Here is what that typically includes:

  • WhatsApp messages: When you tap any WhatsApp button on this site, you are taken to WhatsApp. Whatever name, number, and message content you send there is handled by WhatsApp and received by me as part of our conversation.
  • Email: If you email me, I receive your email address and the content of your message.
  • Project details: When discussing a website, web app, or consultation, you may share business details, ideas, references, or files relevant to the work.

3.2 Public information shown on this site

The website publicly displays my own contact details — my WhatsApp number, email, location, PUBG MOBILE UID, and social links. This is information I have chosen to publish about myself so people can reach me. It is not collected from visitors.

3.3 Technical information

This website itself does not run analytics, does not set its own cookies, and does not log your browsing behaviour. Standard web hosting may keep routine server logs (such as request time and IP address) for security and maintenance, but these are not used to profile you.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

I keep your information private and only share it in limited situations:

  • Service providers: When a project requires it, information may be shared with a hosting provider or other tool needed to deliver the work, on a need-to-know basis.
  • Payment processing: For payments made via M-PESA or bank transfer, the relevant provider processes the transaction details you share with them. Payment account details are shared directly with you when needed and are never published on this website.
  • Legal obligations: If required by law or a legitimate legal process in Kenya, I may disclose information to the extent necessary.

Apart from these cases, your conversations and project details stay between us.

If analytics or other tracking tools are added in the future, this section will be updated and, where appropriate, you will be informed before they are active.

9. Your Rights

Under the Constitution of Kenya and the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have rights over your personal data. In relation to information I hold about you, you may:

  • Be informed about how your data is used (this policy).
  • Access the information I hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Request deletion of your information, where practical.
  • Object to certain uses, or withdraw consent you previously gave.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya if you believe your rights have been breached.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me through WhatsApp or email (section 13). I will respond within a reasonable time, usually within the period set by the Data Protection Act, 2019.
